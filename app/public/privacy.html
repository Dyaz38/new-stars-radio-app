Privacy Policy

New Stars Radio · Last updated: 16 July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how New Stars Radio ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use our listener web app at newstarsradio.com (the "Service"). The Service lets you listen to our live radio stream, view the schedule and station events, like tracks, and see advertisements.

Who we are

New Stars Radio operates an online radio station focused on Hip-Hop and R&B. For privacy questions or requests, contact us at sales@newstarsradio.com.

Information we collect

You can listen without creating an account. We do not ask for your name, email, or password in the listener app.

  • Approximate location. Our servers infer country and sometimes city or region from your IP address to show relevant ads and filter station events.
  • Anonymous identifiers. Random IDs in your browser (stored locally) deliver ads, count impressions and clicks, and sync song likes. These IDs are not linked to your real identity by us.
  • Song likes. If you tap like on a track, we store the artist and title on your device and send an anonymous like record to our server.
  • Reminders and preferences. Volume, reduce motion, event filters, and install choices are saved in your browser.
  • Ad interaction data. When a paid advertisement is shown, our ad server logs impressions and clicks tied to the anonymous user ID and approximate location.
  • Technical logs. Hosting providers may log IP addresses, request times, and browser type for security and reliability.

How we use information

  • Stream the radio and show now-playing metadata
  • Display the programme schedule and station events
  • Serve and measure advertisements
  • Remember your preferences and reminders on your device
  • Improve and protect the Service

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

The Service relies on partners that may collect or process data under their own policies, including Airtime Pro / Icecast (stream and metadata), Vercel and Railway (hosting), Cloudflare R2 (ad images), and public APIs for cover art. When Google AdSense is enabled, Google may show ads and use cookies—see Google's Privacy Policy.

Cookies and similar technologies

Our listener app primarily uses browser storage rather than login cookies. Third parties (especially Google AdSense when active) may set cookies for ad delivery. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Your choices and rights

  • Decline browser notifications in your device settings
  • Clear site data to remove local IDs, likes, and cached content
  • Contact us to ask about access, correction, or deletion of server-held data where applicable law allows

Children

The Service is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Email sales@newstarsradio.com with any privacy question or request.
