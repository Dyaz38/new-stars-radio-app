How to listen

New Stars Radio · Namibia-based online station

New Stars Radio streams in your browser — no account required.

  1. Open newstarsradio.com and press Play to start the live stream.
  2. Check the Schedule for today’s shows and set reminders when you want a heads-up.
  3. Browse Events for station listings (more coming after full launch).
  4. Tap the heart on a track to show you like it.
  5. Use Share on now playing to send a song to a friend.
  6. Install New Stars Radio as an app on your phone or desktop when your browser offers Add to Home Screen / Install (PWA).

In the app, schedule times are shown in your local clock. The station runs on Namibia time (Windhoek, CAT).
