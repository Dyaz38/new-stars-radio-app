About New Stars Radio

Tomorrow's Stars, Today

New Stars Radio is an online station spotlighting up-and-coming artists in Hip-Hop, R&B, and Smooth Jazz. We broadcast live 24/7, publish our programme schedule, and list station events for listeners in Namibia and worldwide.

New Stars Radio is your home for tomorrow's stars, today. We discover independent artists before they hit the mainstream and give them a platform on a live broadcast you can stream from any device.

Our programming blends Hip-Hop, R&B, and Smooth Jazz with presenter-led shows, weekly schedules, and community events. Whether you tune in for the music or the culture, you are hearing curated talent—not an anonymous playlist.

Advertisers and event promoters can reach engaged listeners through our banner placements. For partnerships, advertising, or event listings, contact us by email.

What you can do here

  • Listen to our live stream free of charge
  • View the weekly programme schedule and set show reminders
  • Browse station events and add them to your calendar
  • Like tracks and install the app on your phone for quick access
